How It Works

1

Display the QR Code

Press "Create New QR" from your panel, and display it on a projector or classroom screen.

2

QR Code Refreshes Automatically

The code changes every 10 seconds, so a student cannot take a screenshot and share it with someone else to submit attendance on their behalf.

3

Student Scans with Phone

The QR code, when scanned with a camera, directs the student to a short form where they can enter their name and student ID.

4

View Results Instantly

Track who attended and when they submitted their attendance in real-time; all your past sessions are recorded.

Each instructor has their own account and private QR sessions — you are the only one who can see your sessions and attendance records.
